Privacy Policy

Postera is a personal stamp collection and condition archive application. This Privacy Policy explains how Postera handles information when you use the app on your iPhone.

1. Overview

Postera is designed as a local-first tool. Specimen records, album placement, condition notes, voice memo text, inventory checklists, and attached photos are stored on your device. Postera does not require a cloud account to use core archive features.

2. Information stored on your device

  • Issue titles, country and year text, album/page/cell placement, lifecycle status, and personal notes
  • Condition tags and optional speech-to-text notes created during condition checks
  • Photos you capture with the camera or select from your photo library
  • Inventory progress for albums you choose to check
  • Optional in-app purchase product identifiers and a local archive-credit balance
  • A lightweight local session flag used after Quick Login on the guide screen

3. Permissions

Postera requests system permissions only when you use the related feature:

  • Camera — photograph stamps, avatars, or feedback images
  • Photo Library — choose existing images or save an export preview when you ask
  • Microphone — record condition voice memos
  • Speech Recognition — convert voice memos into editable text notes
  • Notifications — the system may show a one-time authorization prompt; Postera does not schedule or send push notifications after that prompt

You can deny any permission and continue using text-only recording paths. Denied permissions can be changed later in iOS Settings.

4. Purchases

Optional archive packs are processed by Apple In-App Purchase. Apple may collect payment information under Apple Media Services terms. Postera stores only the product identifier and a local credit balance needed to unlock pack features on this device. Postera does not offer a restore-purchases control in the app UI.

5. Exports and sharing

When you export archive data, files are generated locally and handed to the iOS share sheet you choose. Once a file leaves the app through sharing or saving, Postera cannot control copies stored outside the app sandbox.

6. Children

Postera is intended for general audiences who manage personal collections. Do not submit sensitive personal data belonging to children beyond what is necessary for your own archive notes.

7. Hosting of this document

This Privacy Policy page is hosted as a static website. The hosting provider may process standard server logs such as IP address, request time, and user agent for security and delivery.

8. Contact

For privacy questions about Postera, use the support channel listed on the App Store product page once the app is published.
